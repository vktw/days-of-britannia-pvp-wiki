# Britannia Rarities and Whispers of Britannia

!!! success "Status: Live 0.21.0"
    A rare Phoenix and Town Criers offer two new ways to discover events in Britannia.

## Rare Phoenix

A rare Phoenix can appear every hour in one of Britannia's cemeteries. It is a special creature and cannot be tamed.

Carving its untouched corpse can award **Fire Feathers**. The bonus depends on Forensic Evaluation; a higher effective skill increases the chance of receiving a bonus over the normal yield.

## Whispers of Britannia

Speak with a Town Crier to open the conversation for buying a whisper. The price starts at **10,000 gold**, rises after each purchase, and decreases after complete six-hour periods without use, never below the minimum.

Payment can use gold from your backpack and then your account bank. A stale quotation refreshes without charging; a completed purchase is charged even when the rumor provides no useful information.

## What the rumor tells you

- A Phoenix younger than 10 minutes provides no information.
- Between 10 and 15 minutes, the rumor may succeed.
- At 15 minutes and older, the rumor succeeds.
- The result does not reveal location, cemetery, age, or number of Phoenixes.

The goal is to provide a lead, not to replace exploring the world. Check the Town Crier and decide whether the next whisper is worth the price.
