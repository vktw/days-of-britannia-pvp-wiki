This section documents the general map and region rules of the official server.

Felucca is the only public facet of Britannia. Trammel remains reserved for the controlled areas of official arenas.

## Persistent world

Doors, signs, decorations, spawners, vendors, characters and items can be part of the server's persistent world. Code updates do not replace official saves.

Spawns combine the state of the saved world with settings loaded by the server. The actual presence of manually generated or placed objects depends on the current state of the world.
