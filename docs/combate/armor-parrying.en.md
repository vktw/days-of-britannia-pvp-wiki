# Armor and Parrying

!!! success "Status: Live 0.10.2"
    Armor protects the region that is hit; a shield can replace that step entirely.

## Body regions

Each physical hit selects Chest, Legs, Arms, Head, Hands, or Neck. Only armor equipped on that region reduces damage. Ordinary clothing, jewelry, and weapons do not count as regional protection.

The piece's displayed `Physical Resist` indicates how much it absorbs when that region is hit. Better qualities — Defense, Guarding, Hardening, Fortification, and Invulnerability — provide increasing protection.

In PvP, a region without effective armor receives **25% additional damage**. Reactive Armor does not count as an equipped piece. Protection reduces part of the remaining damage, including on uncovered regions.

## Shields

Parrying requires an equipped shield. At 100.0 Parrying, the maximum base block chance is **45% in PvP** and **50% in PvM**.

When a block occurs:

- no body region is selected;
- the shield's Physical Resist absorbs part of the hit;
- Protection, when active, reduces part of the remaining damage;
- final damage never falls below one point.

## What to wear

- Covering every region prevents the uncovered-region bonus.
- Improving one piece protects only its corresponding region.
- A shield adds an independent defense but occupies the second hand.
- Body plate prevents Archery use; Plate Gorgets and shields remain allowed.
