# Patch notes

This page summarizes changes players can notice. Current numbers and full instructions belong on the reference pages.

## 2.6.0 · Breeding the rare mounts

Published on November 11, 2026.

- New female of the rare mounts of Britannia: a mare, an ostard hen or a llama dam, in dark brown and with any of her family's looks (the four horses, the three ostards or the llama). Each family has its own cycle: every **6** to **7** days a new one is born in Felucca, whether others still live or not. She needs **101.0** Animal Taming and moves every **12** hours, and everyone hears when she appears, without the place, and when she is tamed or killed. Each female gives **15** crossings.
- New Vale Clover, dropped by plant creatures (Reaper, Corpser, Bog Thing, Bogling, Swamp Tentacle, Whipping Vine and Treefellow) and, rarely, found while chopping wood in Felucca (at most one an account every **24** hours). One of them and **101.0** Cooking, with an amulet or scroll, make the feed of a crossing.
- New breeding pen: a **500,000** gold deed at the Animal Trainer, one per account, placed by the same rules as a house. It holds a male, a female and an offspring, who do not count as followers and cannot be hurt, behind a gate only the owner opens. When you choose the spot, the pen shows in blue to you alone, with the question "Place the pen here?". After **90** days without the owner in the game, the shrunk animals and the deed go to the bank.
- The breeding's charm: Animal Lore on a rare stallion tells which rare his blood can give and how strong his line is; a foal's tooltip shows its mother, father and stable; you name your stable in the pen's window; when a rare is born everyone hears of it, in the game and on Discord, with its generation; a shrunken mount's statuette shows its lineage; and three new achievements: Rare Blood, Tenth Generation and Master Breeder, which gives the title the Master Breeder.
- Every week a rare female goes up for auction in .auction, shrunk, of a random family and look.
- A tamed mount now obeys any owner, with or without Animal Taming: whoever buys a mount from a tamer uses it as normal.
- Crossing the rare mounts: with **100.0** Herding, Animal Lore and Veterinary and one feed, a male and a female of the same family give, in **72** hours, a tame adult offspring. The offspring has the mother's look and the father's hue, up to **10** numbers above or below, and sometimes it is born in the exclusive hue of the father's band: Obsidian, Ivory, Old Gold, Royal Crimson, Malachite, Sapphire, Amethyst or Glacier, in two tones and with a sparkle, which carries the hue in the name and does not breed. The higher the generation of the father's line, the better the chance of the rare hue.
- New rare event, the Death Rider: now and then, in Felucca, the dead rise in three waves and the necromancer Seris leads them, Odran's scythe in her hands. Beware when she names you a false witness: for a few seconds, the damage you deal her turns on you. Four skulls before the statues at the crypt's entrance show the event is on and light up as each wave falls. Whoever defeats her sees her Skeletal Steed rise and may try to tame it with **100** Animal Taming; the tamer earns the Death Rider achievement and the title the Death Rider. In this event death is real.
- Hall of Echoes: the all-time list now breaks ties by kills, as the event does. The order is wins, then the best stage, then kills, then time, and the line shows the kills ("best 2/10, 9 kills, 20:23").
- Every creature of the Hall of Echoes now belongs to a Slayer group, so the Slayer's Codex, the Arcana's affinity and slayer weapons count against them: each creature keeps its original creature's group, and those that had none take one: the dinosaurs (all but the raptor), Reptilian; the hell cat, Abyss; the echo of the Engine of Nightmares, Elemental. The real Engine of Nightmares, the event's boss, is Elemental too, like its constructs.
- The Bloodmire Bounty's crate now has floors instead of rising one tier: a weapon at least Force, armor or a shield at least Hardening, magic jewelry at least **+5%** in its PvM Mastery bonus and a magic skill amulet at least **+5**. Anything above a floor stays as rolled.
- Commodity deeds can now be sold in .auction: a filled deed goes up with the goods inside; an empty deed is still refused. The auction window is wider, so bids up to **100,000,000** fit.
- The Hall of Echoes' Helm of the Returning Echo shows its right name, without "red" in front, on the helms already dropped too.
- Rare bosses and creatures on the improved AIs (such as the Bloodmire's) no longer pace by a house's steps or back and forth on a Fire Field: their walk now keeps out of the areas they may not enter, and they cross the fire to whoever attacks them.
- Murus Flammae: the wall now hits every creature it passes through, including those running toward you. It rises where you aim and rolls away from you.
- Death Rider: inside the crypt, arrows and spells are no longer blocked by its roof; its outer walls still block. The same stone blocks and stairs no longer block above their height elsewhere in Felucca.
- Two new achievements lead from the starting path to the Arcana: Into the Bloodmire (**3,000** XP), your first kill in the Bloodmire, and First Bloodmire Bounty (**5,000** XP), your first delivered Bounty.

## 2.5.0 · The Hall of Echoes

Published on November 8, 2026.

- Thessaly, the Unbinder, in Nujel'm, trades the magical weapons and armor of the loot for one-use clothing tints in the colour of the magic: Vanquishing or Invulnerability takes **1** piece, Power or Fortification **5**, Force or Hardening **10**, Might or Guarding **15** and Ruin or Defense **20**, plus **50,000** gold per tint. The pieces come from your backpack and bank, bags inside them included, and the trade is whole or nothing. The price of each tint rises **1%** with every trade and falls **1%** for each day without one, never below **50,000**.
- The first tame or the death of a wild Rideable Darkmane is now announced to the whole server and on Discord, with the player's name, **5** minutes later.
- Man in the Hedge: the notice that the true one awaits at the center now reaches only those in the maze, and his death is no longer announced. The event ends with the mount he leaves: its tame or death is announced to the whole server and on Discord, with the winner's name, **5** minutes later.
- Mage, Alchemist and Docks Alchemist now keep at least **640** of each of the eight classic reagents (previously **320**).
- New Donation Chest beside the Nujel'm bank, with the same contents as Britain's: what is given at one can be taken at the other.
- Hall of Echoes, a new solo challenge on Sundays, booked in .events: **24** hours, one run per account, **4** waves and **6** echoes drawn from **10** of the shard's bosses, faithful copies of the originals without their helpers, the same for everyone that day, with a new theme each week. Dying only ends the run, with nothing lost. All the gold goes straight to the bank with a **100%** bonus, and each stage earns Kharag points. One hour before it opens, the event is announced in the game and on Discord.
- Each Sunday's champion takes a trophy and the title the Echo Champion, and the podium earns medals for the talisman slot: gold for the first, silver for the second and bronze for the third. Every creature defeated in the Hall has a **0.5%** chance to leave, straight into the backpack, one of these items in the event's colour: the Dye of the Echoes, a decorative skull or the Helm of the Returning Echo, a Dragon Helm of Invulnerability with a random Mastery at **+10%**.
- Armory: taking out a stack (potions, reagents, arrows, bandages) opens a window that asks how many, with the whole stack filled in.
- Armory: a loadout now also records the weapons, bows and shields you carry in your backpack, bags inside it included. Equipping it brings matching stored pieces back to the backpack, never again the ones you already carry.
- Scrolls of Knowledge of different Masteries now work together: the **+15%** cap (three layers) is the total across all your Masteries. An inactive Mastery is still activated by its scroll, outside the slots. A new scroll no longer replaces another Mastery's bonus: when the total is full it is refused and stays with you.
- Donation Chest: no container goes into the chest any more, not even one placed by staff. A crate full of items counted as a single item, so the chest went past **100** without deleting the oldest.
- Corpser, Reaper, Whipping Vine, Swamp Tentacle, Bogling, Bog Thing and Quagmire are now in the Fey Slayer Group: they count for the group's contracts, achievements and scrolls, and Fey Slayer weapons work on them.
- Armory: a new search field with a Search button. Type part of a name (case and accents do not matter) and press Search: only the matching items show, in the chosen category, each with its own Take. An empty field and Search show everything again.
- The .events window now lists the shard's events, each with its own button: the Free-for-All's opens its registration window, with a button back to the list, and the Hall of Echoes' opens its bookings.

## 2.4.0 · Layers of knowledge and the Map Book

Published on November 6, 2026.

- Scrolls of Knowledge now add up in layers: each scroll used on a Mastery you already know adds a layer of **+5%** for **60** minutes, with its own clock, up to **3** layers (**+15%**). The treasure scroll is worth two layers. Scrolls of the same Mastery now stack, and using a stack lets you choose how many to apply.
- New Treasure Map Book, sold by the Mapmaker for **250,000** Gold: a blessed book that weighs **1** stone and holds up to **50** treasure maps you decoded. Drop a decoded map onto the book and double-click it to see each map's level, facet and position, and take any of them out to your backpack.
- The book has a cursor of its own: aim it at a map, or at one of your containers (the backpack too), and every map you decoded goes into the book, up to **50**. Only what you carry or keep in your bank counts; what is on the ground, in a house or on another character is refused.
- In the book, a map that completes **30** days goes to your bank, with a notice at your first login if you were offline. The window shows beside each map in how many days it leaves, in red in the last **3**.
- The book's window is an open book, like the Slayer's Codex: **six** maps on each page, with level, place, days left and the take button, and arrows to turn the page. A tier filter turns through all the maps and each tier (Stash to Trove), showing how many maps it lists.
- New house Armory: a deed sold by the Architect for **250,000** gold, one per house. The owner and co-owners share its storage and **five** loadouts. It holds weapons, armor, jewelry, clothing, potions, reagents, arrows, bolts, bandages and empty bags. A loadout records the type, material and tier of the pieces, and after a death it wears matching pieces from the storage. Storing yourself or a container opens a list where you can unmark what you want to keep. It does not work in combat (**30** seconds).
- New Protective Ward deed, sold by the Animal Trainer for **50,000** gold: buy as many as you like and use one on your mount, from your backpack or your bank. The next time your mount would die, it is saved and goes to your bank as a statuette, and the ward is spent. It covers every death, PvP included.
- Passus Umbrae now works when you aim straight at a creature: you appear on its tile instead of getting "That location is blocked". Aimed at the ground, nothing changes.
- The first five steps of the starting path (First Hunt, Troll Hunter, Scaled Hunter, Scorpion Hunter and Earth Elemental Hunter) now also give two random Tomes of Expertise each, delivered with the gold. A step whose gold you already received before this version does not give its tomes.
- The craft window of the Blacksmith Armor Kits now shows the real success and exceptional chances: the metal curve (Blackrock at Blacksmith **110** is **91%**, not **70%**) and the **+10%** of a Reliable or Refined tool, which already applied to the roll.
- Aldric's craft orders now pay **+75%** when you made the item yourself and **+50%** when it is exceptional (**225%** of the base with both), up from +50% and +25%.
- New at Kharag: the Prosperity Draught, for **1,000** points. For **60** minutes of play, every PvM kill you share in gives **10%** more of the creature's gold, placed straight in your backpack (in your bank if it is full) with a message, on top of the Young bonus if you have it.
- Edric's crafting trade quests (Blacksmithing, Carpentry, Tailoring, Cooking, Alchemy and Inscription) now count every craft attempt, successful or not, as gathering already did; each quest's total is unchanged, and taming still counts only successes. The quests now give the promised Enduring tool; it was lost on completing the quest.
- The Treasure Map Book has a look of its own, a brown leather book that can be turned, and now takes a book binding, like the Runebook, the Hunter's Compendium, the Slayer's Codex and the Magnum Opus. The Runic Atlas now takes a book binding too, without losing its entries, and can return to its original look with Restore.
- You can now split a stack of Scrolls of Knowledge: double-click it, type the amount and use Split. The new pile keeps its Mastery.

## 2.3.0 · Skills, trades and mounts

Published on November 3, 2026.

- Royal Banking Charter II arrives in the Banker menu: for **150,000** account Gold it permanently raises that character's bank from **250** to **500** items. Only a character that already holds Royal Banking Charter I can buy it.
- New Alchemy Lab for the house, sold by the Alchemist for **250,000** Gold: it works like the public Alchemy Lab in Britain and Serpent's Hold, with the DoB Kegs and the **+7.5%** success bonus, but with no craft fee. The house's owner, co-owners and friends use it from inside the house; visitors cannot. One per house; only the owner can redeed it, and if the house falls the deed goes to the owner's bank.
- The Mage now sells the Runic Atlas for **250,000** Gold: a rune book for **48** runes and up to **100** charges, recharged with Recall scrolls like a Runebook.
- Pets whose shrink statuette was lost (for example, decaying on a house floor) are no longer gone for good: staff can now see them and give each back in a new statuette in its owner's bank.
- Shrink statuettes no longer decay: left on the ground, inside a house or outside, they stay until someone picks them up, like a pet in the stable. Those already on the ground stop decaying too.
- The Season of the Witch creatures now have a Slayer Group: the witches and the Grave Herald are Humanoid, the undead, the Leashed Shade, the Grave Mother and the Ossuary Warden are Undead. Slayers, the Slayer's Codex, Hunter Contracts and Scrolls of Knowledge now work against them.
- In the auction window, each lot's picture, name, bid, time left and button now line up on the same row.
- You can now auction items from your bank: with the bank open, use .auction and pick the item straight from it, even from inside a bag.
- Kharag now trades Giant Beetles in the colors of the DoB ores, from Bronze to Brittanium: **35,000** points for Bronze and **10,000** more for each ore, up to **105,000** for Brittanium. The statuette releases from the backpack a tamed beetle in the ore's color, which keeps it when ridden.
- Kharag now also sells the Hatchet, the Fishing Pole, the Mapmaker's Pen and the Skillet, blessed, with **5,000** charges and a weight of **1** stone, at the same price as the other tools.
- The Arcana grew stronger: Vis now goes up to **180** (it was **100**), enough to chain an ultimate and another ability, or two common abilities such as Carcer Glaciei and Fractura. It holds **60** seconds after a fight before it starts to fall (it was **30**). And casting without enough Vis no longer costs anything: the Arcana just rest **3** seconds. Costs and cooldowns are unchanged. With the updated DoB client, the Vis bar shows up to **180**.
- The Magnum Opus now takes a binding: double-click a boss binding and choose your Magnum Opus. It takes the binding's look and colour, and Turn and Restore work as on the Hunter's Compendium and the Slayer's Codex.
- In the Wild Hunt, Moonmarks now reach hunters up to **24** tiles from the quarry when it falls (it was **18**).
- The Wild Hunt now rides at dawn too: a hunt every **4** hours, at **02:00**, **06:00**, **10:00**, **14:00**, **18:00** and **22:00** (Brasília time).
- In the Wild Hunt's last phase, the Erlking appears once a day at a drawn hunt.
- The grizzly, the unicorn and the Erlking of the Wild Hunt now wear the season's colour, like the Moonlit Hind, and leave the same trail of blood pools, growing as the trail gets fresher. The Erlking's hounds wear the season's colour too and still change colour when they turn solid beside a ward stone.
- Every character, new or old, now has Anatomy, Evaluating Intelligence, Parrying, Fencing and Macing at **100**: bandages heal what they should, spells hit hard, the shield defends and the kryss and maces land from day one. Nobody loses anything.
- The Young gold bonus doubled: it is now half the gold of each creature (it was a quarter). The XP bonus still doubles the XP.
- Fewer swings at thin air against monsters: at **100** weapon skill, the hit chance rose from **80%** to **90%**. Magery's eighth-circle summons (the elementals, Energy Vortex and Summon Daemon) now also succeed **90%** of the time at **100** Magery (it was **80%**).
- New trade quests with Edric, the Trade Instructor, beside the Britain bank: Mining, Blacksmithing, Lumberjacking, Carpentry, Tailoring, Fishing, Cooking, Alchemy, Inscription and Animal Taming. Accepting raises the skill to **30** and gives the trade's tool; doing the task raises it to **60** and gives an Enduring tool of the trade, a Tome of Expertise and **500** PvM Mastery XP. Finishing every trade adds **1,000** gold, **1,000** XP and the title the Jack of All Trades. One quest at a time, each trade once per character.
- Opening a treasure map you can read now also tells the treasure's exact position (x, y). Only whoever can read the map sees it.
- Rideable Darkmane: two Darkmanes in a hundred in the Bloodmire come tamable. It needs **100** Animal Taming, takes one slot and becomes a mount that keeps the colour of the level it was born on. When one is born, the game and Discord announce it.

## 2.2.0 · Auction and craft orders

Published on November 1, 2026.

- Six new rare **Great Harts**, in colors no dye has: Verdigris, Claybloom, Irisveil, Nightsteel, Cinderglass, and Jadeshade. Up to **2** roam the world at once, and every color comes out before any repeats. Each has hunter and tamer achievements; whoever tames all **12** harts earns the title **the Hartbinder**, whoever defeats all **12**, **the Hartbane**.
- Great Hart announcements no longer describe places in the rumor; the only clue to where a hart is is the announcement's own location hint.
- **.rankings** gains a **PVP** tab: it adds up arena duel points for good, System Loot and Personal Loot together (**3** per win, **1** per draw), with wins, draws, and losses, and never resets with the season. The season and its trophies are unchanged, and current points already count.
- New **Leather Armor Kit** in Tailoring, under DoB Armor Kits: with the Royal Armorer's Charter and **75** Inscription, **35** Barbed leather become a deed opening into the **6** leather pieces. Every armor kit now shows who made it, and the pieces it opens into carry that mark too.
- New global **auction** with the **.auction** command: list any tradeable item from your backpack for **24**, **48**, or **72** hours, at the starting price and increment you choose (up to **10** auctions per account). A bid comes from your bank gold and returns if outbid; a bid in the last **5 minutes** extends the auction by **5 minutes**. No one sees who sells or who bids. After the first bid the auction cannot be cancelled; a sale pays the price less a **10%** commission, and an unsold item returns to your bank.
- Auctions now have their own Discord channel: each auction is announced when it opens, again at most once an hour when it gets new bids, and when it ends. No seller or bidder names.
- **Aldric, the Master Artisan**, beside Kharag: every day from **20:00** he asks for one kind of item (kits, Bloodrock and Blackrock weapons, bows, house items, jewels) and takes as many as you deliver, by targeting an item or a container. Each item adds points to your Kharag balance for its materials, **+50%** if you made it and **+25%** if it is exceptional. The next day the items go to auction one at a time; the sale gold leaves the economy, and one that does not sell is discarded.
- Kharag now has rewards for crafters: the **Artisan's Mark**, in **6** colors, colors your name in the "crafted by" line of everything you craft, for good (to change the color, buy another); common tools, blessed, with **5,000** charges and a weight of **1** stone; the **Giant Beetle** and the **Giant Fire Beetle**, found nowhere else, tamed in a statuette; and three single-use dyes in colors of their own: Royal Violet, Twilight Lavender, and Jade.
- Delivering to Aldric's craft orders earns titles: **Apprentice of the Guild** at **100** delivered items, **Journeyman Artisan** at **500**, **Master of Commissions** at **2,000**, and **Grand Artisan of Britannia** at **5,000**, each with its achievement.
- New Huntwarden reward in the Wild Hunt: the **Mantle of the Hunter's Moon**, for **275** Moonmarks, one per account. A mantle in the season's color that, worn by someone with **100** base Tracking, extends Tracking's range by **35** tiles and lists **6** more results (the Trailseeker Cloak gives **25** and **4**; they do not add up). It is not blessed and takes no dye.

## 2.1.0 · The Wild Hunt

Published on October 31, 2026.

- The Wild Hunt gains new quarries from **November 11**: besides the White Hind, the frost-rimed **grizzly**, which tolerates more noise, flees once and not far, and strikes with a telegraphed Charge and Roar, and the pale **unicorn**, which flees in blinks, a glimmer on the ground showing each one first, and strikes with Starfall.
- The White Hind now has less life, so a hunt ends sooner.
- From **November 11** the Wild Hunt's trails gain one or two false branches: they leave an old sign and their freshness falls until a cold sign that leads nowhere. Reading the sequence saves time.
- From **November 21 to 30** the **22:00** hunt is the **Erlking's**: he waits with his spectral pack, names a hunter as his quarry, and looses the hounds on them. The hounds take solid form only near the ward stones, and the Erlking stays beyond reach while his pack runs. At half his life he calls more hounds. Defeating him earns **80** Frostmarks.
- Odran's Condemned Heart and the Man in the Hedge's Eleventh Heart now stack like the other bosses' hearts, including the ones you already have.
- At the Wrong and Hythloth entrances, Thorne, the Bloodmire Stalker, now stands on the other side of the corridor and no longer hides behind the wall.
- **Spirit Speak** no longer heals in PvP: for **2 minutes** after a fight against players, or in an arena fight, the spirits are silent.
- Fighting players no longer costs PvM rewards: whoever has just fought over a spot gets the Mastery XP, the Hart's dye and deed, the Wild Hunt's Frostmarks, and the drops as usual. Masteries still never apply in PvP: for **2 minutes** after a fight against players they do not apply, not even against creatures.
- The Wild Hunt's rewards are now snow white, a color no other item has: the Frostmark, the horn, the Frost Pigment (and what it dyes), the statuette and the Frostmane, Rimewind, and the binding. Those you already own change by themselves, and old Frostmarks still stack with new ones.
- The Wild Hunt's omens and notices now appear in game in your language (Portuguese or English). Before, they were in English only.
- If nobody sights the Wild Hunt's quarry in the first hour, the horn sounds a second and last time with every clue and the den's location, as in the Great Hart announcements. Before, the clues came one an hour.
- The Wild Hunt traded the frost for the storm and the spectral moon: the currency is now the **Moonmark** (the same coin, renamed), the first phase's quarry is the **Moonlit Hind**, the bear is the **storm-scarred grizzly**, and the rewards are the **Moonlight Pigment**, the **Moonmane**, and the **Stormcaller** bow, whose special strike is now a lightning bolt. The Huntwarden traded the snow decorations for hunting trophies: the spectral hound and hind statuettes, the wolf, Great Hart, and grizzly trophies, and **2** bear rugs. What you already own is renamed by itself.
- The Wild Hunt's trail signs now take the quarry's color and grow as the trail warms: **one** piece when cold, **two** when old, and **three** when fresh. They use only large graphics (broken branches, tufts of fur, and blood) and never lie under roofs or against walls.
- Each Wild Hunt hunt now pays more Moonmarks: **50** (it was **40**), plus **10** for an ambush, **10** if the quarry never fled, and **10** if it was sighted before the second hint (**5** each before). The Erlking pays **100** (it was **80**). The Huntwarden's prices are unchanged.
- The Moonlit Hind now has **2,000** life (it was **3,000**).
- Items left on a house's floor no longer decay, even when not locked down. In return they count toward the house's item limit (the storage shown on its sign), with everything they hold, and a full house takes nothing more on its floor.
- **Tracking** now works normally in the Wild Hunt: the quarry appears in the list like any creature when in range. Using Tracking near a trail sign still reads the sign in more detail.
- Inside houses, line of sight now follows the real height of walls, stairs, doors, and floors: the walls and doors of the floor above, steps, and railings no longer block the view of a chest on the floor below, and someone standing in an archway sees normally. Locked-down chests can be used from any tile within reach in every house; a real wall between you and the chest still blocks.
- The Moonlit Hind now wears the season's color, as do its blood and the effects of its attacks. Its trail is made only of pools of its blood in that color: the first signs are **2** pools, growing as the trail gets fresher (**three**, then **four**).
- The Huntwarden's top reward (**575** Moonmarks, one per account) is now the **Visage of the Hunter's Moon**: a deer mask in the season's color, on the model of the dungeon masks, at their highest level (Invulnerability). It replaces the Stormcaller bow in the shop; anyone who already bought the bow keeps it.
- The Huntwarden's shop is remade, everything in the season's color: a bear trap (**30**), an antler chair (**35**), a bearskin rug (**45**), a stuffed wolf (**55**), a bear wall trophy (**75**), the Great Hart wall trophy (**100**), a stuffed bear (**135**), the Moonlight Pigment (**375**), the Moonstrider Statuette (**475**), and the Visage of the Hunter's Moon (**575**). The stuffed animals, the trap, and the chair can be turned; the wall trophies keep their color when taken down. The Horn, the Moonmane, the brown rug, and the statuettes leave the shop; anyone who has them keeps them. The rug's picture in the window now shows whole.
