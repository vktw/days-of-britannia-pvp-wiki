# Classic weapons

!!! success "Status: Live 0.9.1"
The tiers and bonuses below are active on the official server.

| Tier | Damage bonus |
|---|---:|
| Regular | +0 |
| Ruin | +1 |
| Might | +3 |
| Force | +5 |
| Power | +7 |
| Vanquishing | +9 |

The bonus is applied after skill and STR damage scaling and before defensive absorption. The property appears as `Weapon of <tier>`.

## Tactical properties

- One-handed melee weapons can be used with shield.
- Weapons that occupy `TwoHanded` prevent the simultaneous use of shield.
- Polearms and other long weapons configured by the shard can attack range 2.
- Archery has its own range, requires ammunition and releases the prepared shot when the archer stops moving.
- Poison can be applied to eligible melee swords, spears, and knives, in addition to shard-authorized two-handed polearms. Ranged weapons, utility weapons and other two-handed weapons are incompatible.

Special moves remain disabled and do not determine the weapon's poison compatibility.

See [Melee and Archery](../combate/melee-archery.md) and [Poison](../combate/poison.md).
