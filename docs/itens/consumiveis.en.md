# Consumables and utilities

!!! success "Status: Live 0.12.0"
Kit quantities and detailed effects correspond to the official server.

## Potions

Drinkable potions share a 15 second global cooldown. Greater Heal recovers 40 hits and Total Mana recovers 40 mana. Total Refresh and Invisibility take effect immediately.

Poison Potions are not ingested when applied to weapons and do not trigger the cooldown of drinkable potions; they use the flow of the Poisoning skill.

## Bandages

Bandages remove poison immediately without healing health in the same use. Without poison, healing takes 3.5 seconds and reaches 30-40 hits with Healing and Anatomy at 100.0.

## Books and mounts

New characters receive a complete Spellbook, an empty Runebook and a shrunken horse. Spellbooks and Runebooks are preserved when the rest of the inventory is saved for an arena fight.

See [Healing and resources](../combate/cura-recursos.md) for all values and delays.
