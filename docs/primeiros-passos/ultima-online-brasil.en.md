# Ultima Online Brasil: shards, servers, and community

Players searching for **Ultima Online Brasil**, an **Ultima Online Shard Brasil**, or an **Ultima Online Server International** will find a community that has kept Britannia alive for decades. This page introduces the origins of **UO**, the rise of Brazilian freeshards, and the Days of Britannia experience for players from Brasil and other countries.

## What is Ultima Online?

**Ultima Online**, also known simply as **UO**, was released in September 1997 by Origin Systems. The game brought the Ultima series tradition into a persistent online world where many people could explore, fight, trade, build houses, and create their own stories at the same time.

Instead of following a single mandatory campaign, each character could develop skills through use and choose their own routine. This freedom helped establish ideas that would become familiar in MMORPGs: player-driven economies, guilds, conflicts between characters, professions, housing, and community events.

## What do Server, Servidor, and Shard mean?

In Ultima's story, the shattering of the Gem of Immortality produced fragments — shards — that reflected versions of Britannia. The name came to identify each separate persistent world.

In practice, **Server** or **Servidor** describes the service that keeps the world running. **Shard** describes that world's identity: its community, rules, and history. Players often use these terms interchangeably.

A character created on one Shard does not automatically exist on another. Each world maintains its own accounts, characters, economy, and community. This is why it is important to understand the world's direction and consult its own manual when choosing an Ultima Online server.

## How did Ultima Online freeshards emerge?

As Ultima Online grew, fan communities began creating independent worlds known as **freeshards**. These projects were not merely copies: each team could preserve a classic era, adjust the pace of progression, or develop its own interpretation of Britannia.

Two shards based on UO may offer very different experiences. Combat rules, progression, economy, events, and available features belong to the chosen world. Knowing Ultima Online helps, but each Servidor's manual remains the reference for playing there.

## Ultima Online Brasil and Brazilian shards

In the late 1990s and especially during the 2000s, players from **Brasil** organized through forums, websites, chats, and communities to play in Portuguese. Access to the official worlds was Internacional, with barriers such as language, overseas payment, and the distance between the Brazilian community and the available services.

In this setting, each **Brazilian Ultima Online shard** brought together players seeking Portuguese-language support, more convenient event times, and a local community. Some tried to reproduce particular eras of the game; others created their own systems, maps, stories, and PvP or PvM styles.

There is no complete public chronology that reliably identifies a single “first Brazilian freeshard.” Many records lived on forums and pages that later disappeared. The most important legacy is collective: different projects helped keep UO known in the country, formed lasting friendships, and introduced new generations to Britannia.

## Days of Britannia: an Ultima Online Shard Brasil

Days of Britannia is part of this tradition of community-maintained worlds. It is a **Brazilian Ultima Online Shard** with its own direction, PvM content, PvP combat, and documentation for new players. It is not an official Electronic Arts Server and does not represent the official Ultima Online service.

To play, always use the [official Days of Britannia launcher](index.md). Then follow the [getting started guide](index.md), read about your first character, and consult this wiki for the current rules. Experience from another Servidor may help you adapt, but it does not replace information specific to this world.

## Ultima Online Server International and international players

Days of Britannia is developed in Brasil, but it is also friendly to players looking for an **Ultima Online Server International** or a Brazilian shard with accessible English information. The wiki is available in Portuguese and English; on mobile or desktop, use the **PT | EN** buttons in the header to change the page language.

International players can access the same connection, character, combat, item, system, and update information from the [English version of the official wiki](https://dobshard.com/en/). Traditional UO skill, item, and command names are preserved to make communication easier between Brazilian and international players.

The server retains its Brazilian identity. English documentation reduces the entry barrier, lets visitors understand the rules before playing, and welcomes groups from different countries.
