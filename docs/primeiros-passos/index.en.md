# Getting started

This section walks you through installing the official launcher and starting to explore Britannia.

If this is your first contact with the game, start with [Ultima Online Brasil: shards, servers, and community](ultima-online-brasil.md) to understand UO, freeshards, and the Brazilian community.

## Recommended sequence

1. Download and open the [official DOB Launcher](conexao.md).
2. Create your account directly through the login screen.
3. See what the character receives in [Your first character](personagem-inicial.md).
4. Follow [Your first hour in Britannia](primeira-hora.md).
5. Use `.patchnotes` to read the current version within the game.
6. Use the Starter PvP Iron kit to learn combat and `.arena` for a controlled
   fight.
7. Use `.arena` for official challenges or training against PvP Trainer.
