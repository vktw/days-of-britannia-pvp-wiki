# Death and recovery

When you die, choose how to return to [Nujelm Safe Haven](../mundo/nujelm.md):

1. Resurrect immediately in Nujelm; remaining items stay on the corpse.
2. Wait 60 seconds and return to Nujelm with the remaining corpse items recovered.
3. Wait for help for up to five minutes; at the end of the wait, return to Nujelm with the remaining corpse items recovered.

Newcomers use the automatic return described in [Newcomer and protection](newcomer.md).

While the recovery choice or Newcomer countdown is active, the ghost can walk. A ghost within two tiles of a healer can double-click it to request the normal resurrection offer; the healer's range, line of sight, and other conditions still apply.

## Bandage resurrection in 0.49.0

Another player can resurrect you by applying bandages to the **corpse**, not the ghost: each attempt consumes **100 bandages**, success or failure, and the ghost must stay within **2 tiles** of the corpse until it accepts. At **100 Healing** it always works; from 80 Healing and Anatomy the chance starts at **60%**. A carved or skeletal corpse cannot be resurrected this way.

Whoever returns through bandages recovers what was still on the corpse: worn equipment is re-equipped and the rest goes to the backpack; whatever does not fit stays on the corpse. The **Resurrection** spell returns belongings the same way when the ghost accepts within **1 tile** of the corpse. When a resurrection returns every belonging — bandages, Resurrection, return with belongings, or Sacrifice — the empty corpse disappears.

In 0.51.0, returning with belongings through the death menu also re-wears
equipment, and overflow goes to the **bank**, with notice. On resurrection you
get the death's maximum-durability cost, the pieces that broke, and the pieces
close to breaking.

Without a choice, regular players return automatically after five minutes. PvP
deaths allow only a return without corpse items; PvM deaths keep the recovery
options above. Creatures do not loot items from corpses.

!!! success "Status: 0.40.0"
    This flow applies to deaths outside the Arena.
