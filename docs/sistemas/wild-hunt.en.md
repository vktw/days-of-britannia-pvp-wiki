# Season of the Wild Hunt

In November, the **Season of the Wild Hunt** sets hunts in Felucca's wild
regions. From **November 1 to 30**, **4 times a day** (**10:00**, **14:00**,
**18:00**, and **22:00** Brasilia time), the horn names a frost-marked beast;
every hour nobody finds the quarry, a new clue is added. The Huntwarden
serves in the Safe Haven until **December 7**.

## The riddle and the trail

The global English call is a riddle built of fragments: combine its clues to
find the region among the catalog's **51** wild regions. In the region,
follow the trail: hoofprints, broken branches, tufts of fur, and droppings,
fresher near the den.

Double-click a sign (within **3** tiles, with line of sight) to read
freshness, direction, and wind; Tracking near it gives a finer direction, in
**16** directions. The quarry never appears in the Tracking menu.

## The quarry

The quarry senses scent, noise, and large groups: approach against the wind,
without running or riding near it. Startled, it flees to a new den; corner it
by closing escape routes, or wait until it tires. A first strike while it is
calm stuns it. Nobody can harm it from outside the hunt area or from inside a
house.

The first quarry is the **White Hind**: telegraphed frost kicks, leaps at
hunters who strike from afar, and attempts to break the circle.

## Rewards

Each completed hunt pays **Frostmarks** per taking-part account: **40** base,
plus **5** for an ambush, **5** for no flight, and **5** for finding it in the
first hour. The Huntwarden trades **11** rewards:

| Reward | Frostmarks | Account limit |
|---|---:|---|
| Snow Pile | 30 | No specific limit |
| Snow Globe | 35 | No specific limit |
| Brown Bear Rug | 45 | No specific limit |
| Snow Tree | 55 | No specific limit |
| Polar Bear Rug | 75 | No specific limit |
| Snow Statue | 100 | No specific limit |
| Snow Lady Statuette | 135 | No specific limit |
| Horn of the Wild Hunt | 275 | 1 |
| Frost Pigment | 375 | 1 |
| Frostmane Statuette | 475 | 1 |
| Rimewind | 575 | 1 |

The Rimewind is a Vanquishing composite bow with Frostbite (**10%** for **10**
seconds, **40–60** cold against wild PvM creatures). The **Binding of the
First Frost** binding drops at **5%** per quarry.

Season achievements grant overhead titles: **Wild Hunter**, for **25** hunts
(**the Wild Hunter**, **10,000** XP), and **Silent Stalker**, for an
ambush-started, flight-free hunt (**the Silent Stalker**, **5,000** XP).
