# Season of the Wild Hunt

In November, the **Season of the Wild Hunt** sets hunts in Felucca's wild
regions. From **November 1 to 30**, **4 times a day** (**10:00**, **14:00**,
**18:00**, and **22:00** Brasilia time), the horn names a marked beast; every
hour nobody finds the quarry, a new clue is added. The Huntwarden serves in
the Safe Haven until **December 7**.

The season has phases: **1–10**, the First Hunt with the White Hind; **11–20**,
the bear and the unicorn join; **21–30**, the **22:00** hunt is the
**Erlking's**. The currency is the **Moonmark** (the same coin, renamed).

## The riddle and the trail

The global English call is a riddle built of fragments: combine its clues to
find the region among the catalog's **51** wild regions. In the region,
follow the trail: hoofprints, broken branches, tufts of fur, and blood,
fresher near the den.

Signs take the quarry's color and grow as the trail warms: **one** piece when
cold, **two** when old, **three** when fresh (large graphics only, never
under roofs or against walls). From **November 11**, trails gain **one or two
false branches**, leaving an old sign and fading to a cold sign that leads
nowhere.

Double-click a sign (within **3** tiles, with line of sight) to read
freshness, direction, and wind; **Tracking** works normally in the hunt (the
quarry appears in the list when in range), and Tracking near a sign reads it
in more detail. The quarry never appears alone in the Tracking menu.

## The quarries

The quarry senses scent, noise, and large groups: approach against the wind,
without running or riding near it. Startled, it flees to a new den; corner it
by closing escape routes, or wait until it tires. A first strike while it is
calm stuns it. Nobody can harm it from outside the hunt area or from inside a
house.

- **White Hind** (until day 10) and **Moonlit Hind** (after): telegraphed
  frost kicks, leaps at hunters who strike from afar, and attempts to break the
  circle. The Moonlit one has **2,000** life; its trail is only pools of its
  own blood (**2** cold pools, **3** old, **4** fresh).
- **Frost-rimed grizzly** (from day 11, storm-marked): tolerates more noise,
  flees once and not far, and strikes with an announced Charge and Roar.
- **Pale unicorn** (from day 11): flees in leaps, a glimmer on the ground
  before each one, and strikes with Starfall.
- **Erlking** (**22:00** hunt, days **21–30**): waits with the spectral pack,
  names a hunter as quarry, and looses the hounds. The hounds only hold solid
  form near the ward stones, and the Erlking stays out of reach while his pack
  runs. At half his life he calls more hounds. Defeating him pays **100**
  Moonmarks.

## Rewards

Each hunt pays **Moonmarks** per taking part account: **50** base, plus
**10** for an ambush, **10** with no flight, and **10** for finding it before
the second hint (previously **40** and **5** each). The Huntwarden trades
**11** rewards, with proper names and deeds that keep the name:

| Reward | Moonmarks | Account limit |
|---|---:|---|
| Snare of the Wild Hunt (bear trap) | 30 | No specific limit |
| Erlking's Antler Throne (antler chair) | 35 | No specific limit |
| Pelt of the Storm Bear (bear rug) | 45 | No specific limit |
| Moonhowl, the Mounted Wolf (stuffed wolf) | 55 | No specific limit |
| Head of the Storm Bear (bear trophy) | 75 | No specific limit |
| Antlers of the Moonlit Hart (Great Hart trophy) | 100 | No specific limit |
| Stormhide, the Mounted Grizzly (stuffed bear) | 135 | No specific limit |
| Mantle of the Hunter's Moon (+35 Tracking mantle) | 275 | 1 |
| Moonlight Pigment | 375 | 1 |
| Moonstrider Statuette (season ostard) | 475 | 1 |
| Visage of the Hunter's Moon (Invulnerability mask) | 575 | 1 |

Stuffed animals, the trap, and the chair can be turned; trophies keep their
color when taken down; the rug shows whole in the window. The **Mantle of the
Hunter's Moon** needs **100** base Tracking to wear (range **+35** tiles,
**+6** results; does not add with the Trailseeker), is not blessed, and takes
no dye. The **Visage** replaces the Stormcaller bow (buyers keep it).

The **Binding of the First Frost** binding drops at **5%** per quarry. If
nobody sights the quarry in the first hour, the horn sounds a second and last
time with every clue and the den.

Season achievements grant overhead titles: **Wild Hunter**, for **25** hunts
(**the Wild Hunter**, **10,000** XP), and **Silent Stalker**, for an
ambush-started, flight-free hunt (**the Silent Stalker**, **5,000** XP).
