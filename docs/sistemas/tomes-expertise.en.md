# Tomes of Expertise

**Tomes of Expertise** are progression rewards found in PvM loot. Using a tome
grants permanent progress in one of the 49 active skills.

## Where to find them

- Eligible PvM creatures have a **7.5% chance** to drop a tome.
- Animals are excluded from this drop.
- Tamed animals, creatures that were previously tamed, and summons also do not
  generate this reward.

The tome appears in the creature's loot and follows the corpse's normal
lifetime. Open the corpse before it disappears.

## How much a skill can advance

Most tomes grant a small gain. High values are rare and should not be treated
as guaranteed outcomes.

Tomes from the **Craft** and **Harvesting** families grant at most **1.0** skill.
Progress is permanent and respects the current limit of the selected skill.

## How to use one

Keep the tome in your backpack, set the listed skill to raise, and double-click
it. The tome is consumed when the gain is applied.

For other hunting rewards, see [PvM rewards](../itens/recompensas-pvm.md).
