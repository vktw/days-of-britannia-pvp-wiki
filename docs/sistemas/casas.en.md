# Houses

## Private-house boundary

Authorized players inside private houses are protected from external hostile
actions and cannot attack across the house boundary. Public houses remain
unchanged.

## Visible content in private houses

House content can also display objects placed in a private house. This
visibility does not grant outside interaction: containers, maps, and other
non-structural objects remain protected from removal, use, or transfer by
players without access.

## Same-floor interactions

Structural floors do not block interactions on the same floor when they are
below the line of sight. Walls, doors, other floors, and access rules remain
protected.

## Co-owner actions in 0.45.0

Co-owners can open and release chests locked down or secured by the owner,
except when the chest's security is set to **Owner**. They can also use Moving
Crates, toggle teleporters and public/private status, customize or convert the
house, change its sign, and use addons and decorations.

Transferring or demolishing the house, changing its model, managing vendors or
the co-owner list, and accessing chests with **Owner** security remain
owner-only actions.

In 0.51.0, **wooden boxes** (and similar reward boxes) only go on the floor of
a house you own or co-own. Already placed boxes stay where they are.

## Magincia in 0.49.0

Placing a house in **Magincia** no longer crashes the server, but the attempt
stays refused: building houses in the town is not allowed.

## Floor items and line of sight in 2.1.0

In 2.1.0, items left on the house floor no longer decay over time, even when
not locked down. In return, they count toward the house's item limit (the
storage on its sign), with everything they hold; a full house takes nothing
more on its floor.

Inside houses, line of sight follows the real height: the walls and doors of
the floor above, steps, and railings no longer block the view of a chest on
the floor below; someone standing in an arch or on a threshold sees normally.
Use locked-down chests from any tile within reach in every house; a real wall
between you and the chest still blocks.

## Commodity Deed Box

Bankers sell the **Commodity Deed Box for 20,000 Gold**. To operate Commodity
Deeds, the box must be **secured inside a house**; keeping it in your backpack
is not enough.
