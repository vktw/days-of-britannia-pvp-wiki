# Player Vendors

Nujelm's public market lets you place a protected **Player Vendor** on one of
42 reserved wooden benches inside Felucca's Safe Haven.

## How to place one

1. Obtain a compatible employment contract.
2. Go to a reserved wooden bench in Nujelm's market.
3. Use the contract and confirm placement at the available location.

Each account and public IP may keep **one Nujelm vendor**. This limit does not
change Player Vendors placed inside houses.

## Fees and reserve

The vendor charges the system's native daily fee plus **1,500 Gold**. Placement
requires an initial three-day reserve funded from the bank:

- empty vendor: **4,680 Gold** initial reserve;
- the real cost may rise with the stock;
- the vendor menu shows its daily cost and reserve balance.

The vendor remains fixed and protected while occupying its reserved bench.

## When Gold runs out

Insufficient funds start a **24-hour** grace period. During that time:

- existing stock remains for sale;
- new stock additions are blocked;
- the owner may deposit Gold from the bank at login.

If grace expires, stock and Gold return to the bank with overflow handling. The
account must wait **seven days** before placing another Nujelm vendor.

See [Nujelm Safe Haven](../mundo/nujelm.md) for protection rules and
[Royal Banking Charter](royal-banking-charter.md) to expand the bank.
