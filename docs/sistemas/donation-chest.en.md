# Donation Chest

Beside the Britain bank stands the **Donation Chest**, a golden metal chest
for trading items with other players: leave what you do not want and take
what you like, with no limit. In 2.5.0, a second chest opened beside the
Nujel'm bank, with the same contents: what is given at one can be taken at
the other.

It holds **100** items (a stack counts as **1**); when the **101st** goes in,
the oldest disappears. It takes no gold, checks, containers, blessed items, or
untradeable items — not even placed by staff.

!!! success "Status: 1.3.0"
    Free item exchange in Britain.
