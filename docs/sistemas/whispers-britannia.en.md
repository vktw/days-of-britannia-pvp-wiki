# Whispers of Britannia

**Whispers of Britannia** turn Town Criers into a source of leads for rare
events. A rumor guides exploration, but does not replace discovering the world
or publish coordinates.

## Buying a whisper

Speak with a Town Crier to open the conversation for buying a whisper. The price
starts at **10,000 gold**, rises after each purchase, and decreases after
complete six-hour periods without use, never below the minimum.

Payment can use gold from your backpack and then your account bank. A stale
quotation refreshes without charging; a completed purchase is charged even when
the rumor provides no useful information.

## What the rumor tells you

- A Phoenix younger than 10 minutes provides no information.
- Between 10 and 15 minutes, the rumor may succeed.
- At 15 minutes and older, the rumor succeeds.
- The result does not reveal location, cemetery, age, or number of Phoenixes.

Town Criers can also share rumors about Rare Events and the **Rare Rideable
Llama**. The goal is to provide a lead, not to replace exploring the world.

## Whispers interface

The Whispers gump is wider and provides more room to read the rumors.

See [Rare spawns](spawns-raros.md) for the creatures and events that can be
found during exploration.
