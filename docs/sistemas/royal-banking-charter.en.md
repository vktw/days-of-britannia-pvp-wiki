# Royal Banking Charter

**Royal Banking Charter I** permanently expands the character's bank capacity
from **125 to 250 items**.

## How to purchase it

1. Open a Banker's context menu.
2. Choose the Royal Banking Charter I option.
3. Confirm the **50,000 Gold** payment from the account balance.

The expansion belongs to the character who purchased it. It is permanent and
does not need to remain as an item in the backpack.

## What does not change

- Saying `bank` still opens only the normal bankbox.
- Ordinary physical Gold and Bank Checks continue converting into the account
  balance through their current flows.
- The Charter raises the item limit; it does not add a second bankbox.

The expansion is especially useful for maintaining [Player Vendor](player-vendors.md)
stock or storing crafting materials.
