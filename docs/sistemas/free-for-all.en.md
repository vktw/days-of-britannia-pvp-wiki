# Free-for-All

Every week, the **Free-for-All** takes players to the Britain Cemetery in
Trammel: **Sunday at 21:00** and **Wednesday at 12:15**. Everyone fights
everyone with the arena Iron kit, anonymous under the same shroud and the same
name. At least **2** players are required, or the event is cancelled.

## How to join

Register with `.events` from **15 minutes** before the time. Your belongings
are kept safe and come back when you leave the event.

## Fight rules

- No summons. Teleport works inside the cemetery, but not to leave it.
- After **20 minutes** of fighting comes **sudden death**: nobody can hide any more and everyone loses more and more life until one remains. If the last ones fall to the same drain, the one with the most life wins and, if tied, the one who dealt the most damage.
- Whoever comes back from the event is protected for **5 seconds**: no spell cast in the fight reaches them at home.
- The last one standing receives the **DoB FFA Trophy** in the bank and **the Last Contender** title. Achievements count your fights (**1**, **10**, and **50**) and wins (**1**, **5**, and **25**) in the event.
- Event announcements follow each player's language.
