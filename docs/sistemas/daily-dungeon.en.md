# Daily Dungeon

The Daily Dungeon is the day's best reference for combining PvM with Mastery progression. The selection changes on a daily rotation; use `.dailydungeon` to see the current dungeon and the remaining time.

## What changes in the daily dungeon

- Eligible creatures grant a **25% PvM Mastery experience bonus**.
- The frequency of customized Loot Packs for eligible creatures is **doubled**.
- The bonus lasts until the next daily rotation.

The bonuses apply to the dungeon selected at that moment. Check the command window before planning your route.

## Lost Lands access

Common player access to Lost Lands and its dungeons is closed under the Live ruleset. Identify the Daily Dungeon through the command and the in-game signs; do not assume that a dungeon outside the rotation offers the same bonuses.

## Next step

Open `.dailydungeon` before heading out for PvM, then combine the rotation with your [PvM Mastery](pvm-mastery.md) build and [PvM rewards](../itens/recompensas-pvm.md).
