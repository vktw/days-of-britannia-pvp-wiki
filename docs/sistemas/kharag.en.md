# Kharag, Heart of the Earth

At the **Nujel'm Safe Haven**, **Kharag, Heart of the Earth**, accepts full
**Commodity Deeds** of ingots, logs, or boards for points. Points buy
draughts, dyes, and statuettes. Double-click Kharag within **3 tiles** with
the deeds in your backpack to donate and check your balance.

## Points per resource

Each deed unit is worth points by resource, with each log matching its
same-wood board:

| Resource | Points per unit |
|---|---:|
| Iron and normal wood | 0.1 |
| Bronze | 1.0 |
| Oak | 1.3 |
| Shadow | 1.6 |
| Ash | 2.1 |
| Silver | 2.5 |
| Yew | 3.1 |
| Gold | 4.2 |
| Bloodrock, Heartwood, Bloodwood, and Frostwood | 6.3 |
| Blackrock | 8.3 |
| Mythril | 12.5 |
| Brittanium | 25.0 |

Only full, canonical deeds earn points; loose ore and lookalikes are refused.

## Rewards

Draughts last **60 minutes** of play.

| Reward | Points | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Hunter's Draught | 150 | Doubles PvM Mastery XP |
| Fortune Draught | 300 | Raises loot pack rolls |
| Craft Draught (10 trades) | 150 | +5% crafting success in the trade |
| Exceptional Draught (6 trades) | 300 | +10% Exceptional in the trade |
| Runebook dye (1 use) | 600 | Dyes 1 runebook or recall rune in your backpack |
| Ore elemental statuette | 1,000–15,000 | House decoration, by metal |

Craft Draughts cover Blacksmithy, Tailoring, Carpentry, Tinkering, Alchemy,
Inscription, Fletching, Cooking, Cartography, and Mining. Exceptional
Draughts cover Blacksmithy, Tailoring, Carpentry, Tinkering, Fletching, and
Inscription. Statuettes run from Iron (1,000) to Brittanium (15,000), through
Bronze, Shadow, Silver, Gold, Bloodrock, Blackrock, and Mythril.
