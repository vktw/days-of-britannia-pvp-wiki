# Season of the Witch

In October, the **Season of the Witch** brings a coven of five witches to the
cemeteries of Felucca. Spot the coven, survive the undead waves, defeat the
special foe and the leader, collect **Coven Orbs**, and find the
**Coven Keeper at the Nujel'm Safe Haven** to claim the edition's rewards.

The season starts on **October 1**: decorations and the Coven Keeper arrive
after 00:00 BRT and the first coven appears at the **10:00** slot. The guard
removal in Jhelom, Nujel'm, and the new Britain and Minoc strips already
applies since deployment on September 29.

## The coven

A single coven holds one Felucca cemetery at a time: Britain, Cove, Jhelom,
Moonglow, Nujel'm, Vesper, or Yew. It is discovered by sight — seeing one
witch with line of sight is enough. The first valid aggression activates the
fight, including pet and summon attacks, and all five witches retaliate at
once. Each witch has a role: the **guardian** shields a sister, the
**mender** heals wounds, and the **summoner** calls larger waves; the rest
fight as common witches.

A red global notice announces each coven without revealing the cemetery and
repeats each hour while it lasts; another announces victory or fading. The
coven does not appear in Whispers of Britannia.

Undead waves arrive as each witch loses part of her health. Summoned undead
fall to a single blow, and a **Grave Champion** may rise with the waves,
always carrying a PvM Loot Pack reward. The ritual circle holds its fighters:
striking from outside or fleeing mid-fight drags the adventurer back inside.
Coven creatures are immune to paralysis, and each hit they take from a
summoned creature has a 30% chance to dispel the summon.

The first fallen witch releases one of three special foes.
**The Ossuary Warden** marks the impact radius of his strike on the ground.
The **Leashed Shade** marks where its curse will land: move more than three
tiles from the mark, break line of sight, or damage it during the warning to
prevent application — once applied, the curse completes its four 15-damage
pulses. The **Grave Herald** opens the graves to call Skeletal Knights;
damaging him interrupts the ritual.

After the five witches and the special foe, one of four leaders appears:
**The Candle Widow**, **The Grave Mother**, **The Nameless Crone**, and
**The Veiled Matriarch**. Extinguish the candles to weaken the Candle Widow
and the Matriarch, and leave the ground marks to avoid the leaders' rituals.
Leaders attack pets and summons normally, but their special abilities seek an
eligible adventurer when the current target is a controlled creature.

Any adventurer may join: there is no loot reservation for the first attacker.
The leader's death dissipates the remaining undead with no reward — already
created corpses and loot remain — and twelve freed souls rise at the cemetery.
After victory, another coven appears at the next fixed Brasilia time:
**10:00 and 22:00** during The Missing Names (October 1–7), **02:00, 10:00,
14:00 and 22:00** during The Gathering (October 8–21), and **02:00, 06:00,
10:00, 14:00, 18:00 and 22:00** during The Sabbath (October 22–31).

## Currency and redemption window

The Coven Orb is the edition's currency and belongs to it: only current-season
Orbs pay for the Coven Keeper's rewards. Each witch grants **2** Orbs, the
special foe **3**, the leader **5**, and each supporting undead **1**.
Defeating the leader quickly grants extra Orbs. Each special foe and each
leader also grants one additional PvM Loot Pack roll, and magical weapons and
armor from the leader's loot rise one quality tier.

The Coven Keeper serves at the **Nujel'm Safe Haven**, beside the Season of
Thorns Briarheart Keeper, through October and during Redemption week
(November 1–7). Account limits apply per edition.

Witchfire Pigment is the seasonal dye, Hallowmare is the edition's mount, and
The Third Offering is the reward weapon, whose Witchfire power can call a
column of flame against wild creatures. The Witchfire Cauldron is a Cooking
station with its own fire and unlimited uses. The three top rewards share the
season's metallic pumpkin hue. Limits control redemption; rewards remain
tradeable and lootable.

Leaders may leave **The Unwritten Chronicle** with a **10%** chance: a
collectible book whose pages do not yet reveal the story. It is never required
to attack, participate, or claim rewards.

## October in Britannia

The seven cemetery towns gain dark decorations that grow through October, and
Trinsic and Minoc get a touch of Halloween. Restless spirits rarely appear and
fade in the streets (1% per town per minute early on, 3% mid-month, 6% late).
