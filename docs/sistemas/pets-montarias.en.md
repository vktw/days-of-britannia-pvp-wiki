# Pets and mounts

This page gathers the shard-specific rules for storing pets, finding rare
mounts, and releasing controlled creatures. Exact event locations remain part
of exploration. A tamed mount obeys any owner, with or without Animal Taming.

## Shrink Potion

Tamed pets controlled by the character can be stored with a **Shrink Potion**.
The potion must be in the backpack: use it and select the pet. The same animal,
including its appearance, is preserved as a statuette and occupies no follower
slots while stored.

A pet cannot be shrunk:

- when dead, summoned, or owned by another character;
- during Arena, combat, recent aggression, or a pet transfer;
- while the character is dead or has a trade window open.

To restore it, keep the statuette in the backpack and double-click it. The
character must be alive, outside the Arena, and free from combat, recent
aggression, or trade, with enough follower slots and physical space at the
location. The pet returns under control with the follow order.

Shrink statuettes do not decay: left on the ground, inside a house or outside,
they stay until someone picks them up, like a pet in the stable. Pets from
lost statuettes are not gone for good: call staff, who returns each one in a
new statuette in your bank.

## Protective Ward in 2.4.0

The **Protective Ward** is a deed sold by the Animal Trainer for **50,000**
gold: buy as many as you like and use one on your mount, from your backpack or
your bank. The next time it would die, it is saved and goes to your bank as a
statuette, and the ward is spent. It covers every death, PvP included.

## Rare mounts

| Group | Possibilities | Minimum Animal Taming | Behavior |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Rare Britannia | Llama, Mustang, or Ostard, with body and color variations | 90.0 | Keeps one wild mount active in an accessible area of pre-T2A Felucca wilderness. Once tamed or killed, it gives way to a new cycle. |
| Fixed events | Rare Rideable Llama | 60.0 | Appears through its own event and may be announced by Town Criers. |
| Fixed events | Rare Desert, Forest, Frenzied, or Zostrich Ostard | 70.0 | Each family has its own event. A tamed mount frees its slot for the next cycle. |
| Odran | Ruby Mustang | 95.1 | Released after Odran is defeated and protected from wild creatures for 60 seconds. |

Rare Britannia mounts use colors from the ordinary dye range. Frenzied,
Zostrich, and Forest Ostard statuettes preserve the animal's original color.

For clues without revealing coordinates, see [Rare spawns](spawns-raros.md) and
[Whispers of Britannia](whispers-britannia.md).

## Rare females in 2.6.0

Rare mounts gained tamable females: a mare, an ostard hen or a llama dam, in
dark brown and with any of her family's looks. Each family has its own cycle:
every **6** to **7** days a new one is born in Felucca, whether others still
live or not. She needs **101.0** Animal Taming, moves every **12** hours, and
everyone hears when she appears, without the place, and when she is tamed or
killed. Each female gives **15** crossings. Every week a rare female goes up
for auction in `.auction`, shrunk, of a random family and look.

## Breeding pen in 2.6.0

The **breeding pen** is a **500,000** gold deed at the Animal Trainer, one per
account, placed by the same rules as a house. It holds a male, a female and an
offspring, who do not count as followers and cannot be hurt, behind a gate only
the owner opens. When you choose the spot, the pen shows in blue to you alone,
with the question "Place the pen here?". After **90** days without the owner
in the game, the shrunk animals and the deed go to the bank.

## Crossing in 2.6.0

With **100.0** Herding, Animal Lore and Veterinary and one feed, a male and a
female of the same family give, in **72** hours, a tame adult offspring. The
feed takes **1** Vale Clover — dropped by plant creatures (Reaper, Corpser,
Bog Thing, Bogling, Swamp Tentacle, Whipping Vine and Treefellow) and rarely
found while chopping wood in Felucca (at most one an account every **24**
hours) — and **101.0** Cooking, with an amulet or scroll counting. The
offspring has the mother's look and the father's hue, up to **10** numbers
above or below, and sometimes it is born in the exclusive hue of the father's
band: Obsidian, Ivory, Old Gold, Royal Crimson, Malachite, Sapphire, Amethyst
or Glacier, in two tones and with a sparkle, which carries the hue in the name
and does not breed. The higher the generation of the father's line, the better
the chance of the rare hue.

## Lineage and achievements in 2.6.0

Animal Lore on a rare stallion tells which rare his blood can give and how
strong his line is. A foal's tooltip shows its mother, father and stable, and
you name your stable in the pen's window. When a rare is born everyone hears of
it, in the game and on Discord, with its generation, and a shrunken mount's
statuette shows its lineage. There are three new achievements: **Rare Blood**,
**Tenth Generation** and **Master Breeder**, which gives the title **the
Master Breeder**.

## General rules

- Controlled pets and player summons cannot attack or damage other players but
  remain available in PvM.
- Tamed, formerly tamed, and summoned creatures grant no Gold, XP, death loot,
  or PvM progress.
- This ruleset does not use pet bonding.

## Releasing a pet

The game requests confirmation before releasing a pet. Summons and naturally
aggressive pets disappear permanently; aggressive pets take all carried items
with them. Passive or reactive pets remain in the world normally after losing
their bond to the former owner.

## Taming at 100.0 or more in 1.3.0

With **100.0** or more Animal Taming, taming progress shows above the creature
and the tame is guaranteed when the bar completes: each completed failed
attempt adds the roll's effective chance, and the attempt that fills the bar
succeeds. Progress is private, pauses below 100, and lasts **1 hour** since
the last attempt.
