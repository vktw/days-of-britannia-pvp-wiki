# Pets and mounts

This page gathers the shard-specific rules for storing pets, finding rare
mounts, and releasing controlled creatures. Exact event locations remain part
of exploration.

## Shrink Potion

Tamed pets controlled by the character can be stored with a **Shrink Potion**.
The potion must be in the backpack: use it and select the pet. The same animal,
including its appearance, is preserved as a statuette and occupies no follower
slots while stored.

A pet cannot be shrunk:

- when dead, summoned, or owned by another character;
- during Arena, combat, recent aggression, or a pet transfer;
- while the character is dead or has a trade window open.

To restore it, keep the statuette in the backpack and double-click it. The
character must be alive, outside the Arena, and free from combat, recent
aggression, or trade, with enough follower slots and physical space at the
location. The pet returns under control with the follow order.

## Rare mounts

| Group | Possibilities | Minimum Animal Taming | Behavior |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Rare Britannia | Llama, Mustang, or Ostard, with body and color variations | 90.0 | Keeps one wild mount active in an accessible area of pre-T2A Felucca wilderness. Once tamed or killed, it gives way to a new cycle. |
| Fixed events | Rare Rideable Llama | 60.0 | Appears through its own event and may be announced by Town Criers. |
| Fixed events | Rare Desert, Forest, Frenzied, or Zostrich Ostard | 70.0 | Each family has its own event. A tamed mount frees its slot for the next cycle. |
| Odran | Ruby Mustang | 95.1 | Released after Odran is defeated and protected from wild creatures for 60 seconds. |

Rare Britannia mounts use colors from the ordinary dye range. Frenzied,
Zostrich, and Forest Ostard statuettes preserve the animal's original color.

For clues without revealing coordinates, see [Rare spawns](spawns-raros.md) and
[Whispers of Britannia](whispers-britannia.md).

## General rules

- Controlled pets and player summons cannot attack or damage other players but
  remain available in PvM.
- Tamed, formerly tamed, and summoned creatures grant no Gold, XP, death loot,
  or PvM progress.
- This ruleset does not use pet bonding.

## Releasing a pet

The game requests confirmation before releasing a pet. Summons and naturally
aggressive pets disappear permanently; aggressive pets take all carried items
with them. Passive or reactive pets remain in the world normally after losing
their bond to the former owner.
