# Arcana

The **Arcana** are active PvM abilities in **six** schools — **Fire**,
**Frost**, **Life**, **Shadow**, **Blood**, and **Void** — each with **three**
abilities and one ultimate, gathered in the new **Magnum Opus** book. Using
them requires the updated DoB client.

## How to begin

Speak to **Maelis Greyveil** in the Nujelm Safe Haven (you need the
**Slayer's Codex**): choose a first school, receive the Magnum Opus with its
first ability, and prove it in **10** PvM kills using that school. The reward
is **5,000** XP of the school and the **Initiate** achievement, with the
title **the Initiate**. Afterwards, she opens each new school for **100,000**
gold.

Other abilities come from **Arcana Scrolls**, dropped in PvM by the creatures
of the group weak to the school. Scrolls can be traded and teach only in an
open school; the scroll of an ability you already know gives the school's
**Essence** (**+10%** to its abilities for **60 minutes** of play).

| School | Weak group |
|---|---|
| Fire | Undead and Arachnid |
| Frost | Reptilian |
| Life | Abyss |
| Shadow | Humanoid |
| Blood | Fey |
| Void | Elemental |

## Vis and the bar

**Vis** is a charge of up to **100** that rises as you deal or take damage
from creatures and as you heal allies in PvM, shown as the fourth row of the
health bar. Each ability spends Vis and has a cooldown; without enough Vis it
fails and spends what you had.

The **bar** holds **4** abilities of any school and **1** ultimate, which
requires **three** abilities of its school on the bar. Drag the book's icons
onto the client's hotkeys. The bar only changes after **30 seconds** out of
combat.

## Progression

Each PvM kill gives Mastery XP to the schools used in the fight. With the XP,
buy levels with gold and put each into an ability, with no maximum level. At
levels **25** and **50** the ability gains a larger area, duration, or effect
and a new animation.

Each Slayer Group is weak to one school (**+25%** ability damage) and resists
another (**-25%**), shown in the creature's tooltip beside the Slayer Group.

## The schools

| Group | Weak to | Resists |
|---|---|---|
| Undead | Fire | Blood |
| Arachnid | Fire | Shadow |
| Reptilian | Frost | Fire |
| Abyss | Life | Void |
| Humanoid | Shadow | Life |
| Fey | Blood | Life |
| Elemental | Void | Frost |

- **Fire**: Pluvia Ignea (Ember Rain) — rain over an area; Murus Flammae (Flame Wall) — rolling wall that leaves Ablaze; Eruptio Ignis (Fireburst) — explosion that detonates Ablaze; Ruina Caeli (Meteor) — meteor with burning ground.
- **Frost**: Carcer Glaciei (Ice Prison) — freezes the surroundings; Fractura (Shatter) — shatters, bigger on frozen, and breaks freeze; Campus Pruinae (Frost Field) — slowing field with cold; Hiems Aeterna (Blizzard) — blizzard that freezes after 4 seconds inside.
- **Life**: Circulus Sanationis (Healing Circle) — area heal for allies and pets; Scutum Lucis (Aegis) — shield against creatures; Purgatio (Purify) — cures poison and curse and hurts Undead and Abyss; Renascentia (Rebirth) — resurrects a PvM ghost and heals the living.
- **Shadow**: Signum Umbrae (Shadow Mark) — mark that amplifies party damage; Passus Umbrae (Shadow Step) — teleports and strikes three times; Caecitas (Blindness) — blinds (misses); Eclipsis (Eclipse) — damage with darkness (more party damage, miss chance).
- **Blood**: Missio Sanguinis (Bloodletting) — drain that heals the caster; Pactum Sanguinis (Blood Pact) — trades 20% of max life for Vis, no cursor; Coagulum (Clot) — reduces creature damage; Exsanguinatio (Exsanguination) — area drain that heals the party.
- **Void**: Tractus Vacui (Void Pull) — pulls close and taunts; Concha Vacui (Void Shell) — absorbs damage and zaps adjacents; Puteus Gravitatis (Gravity Well) — roots with energy; Singularitas (Singularity) — pulls, roots, and collapses.

## PvM only

The Arcana do not affect players, the PvP Trainer, arenas, or the Free-for-All,
do not work in the Safe Haven, and cannot be used until **2 minutes** after
combat against a player.

In the client's Options, the **Arcana** section chooses how the effects appear:
**Full** shows everything, animated; **Static**, only the marks on the ground,
held still; **Tiles**, only the tiles hit, in the school's color.
