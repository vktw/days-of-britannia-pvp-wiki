# Season of Thorns

In September, **Season of Thorns** brings Overgrown creatures and seasonal
rewards to PvM exploration. Defeat these creatures, collect **Heart of the
Briar**, and visit the **Briarheart Keeper in Nujelm Safe Haven** to redeem
the edition's rewards.

## Overgrown creatures

Ettins, Trolls, Frost Trolls, Ogres, Ogre Lords, Cyclopes, and Minotaurs can
produce an Overgrown creature after an eligible death. Growing vegetation
signals its arrival; not every kill triggers a transformation.

Overgrown still participate in normal PvM systems, including Mastery and hunts.
Their loot neither replaces nor duplicates the original creature's loot.
**Overgrown Grasp** can root you for **3 seconds** and apply Deadly Poison.

Wild **Arctic Ogre Lords** can also return as Overgrown while preserving their
icy identity.

The **Ancient Overgrowth** variant has a **10% chance** to drop the bilingual
collectible book **O Testamento da Primeira Sarça**.

## Currency and redemption window

Heart of the Briar is tradable and lootable. A normal Overgrown awards **1**
unit; an Ancient awards **3**. Currency belongs to its original edition: old
currency cannot pay for rewards in a future edition.

The Briarheart Keeper is available during September and for **7 days after the
natural end**. Redeem your currency within this window. Account limits are
shared across characters and reset each edition.
