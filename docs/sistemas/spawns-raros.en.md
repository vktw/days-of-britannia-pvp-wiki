# Rare spawns

Britannia has rare creatures and events that reward exploration. The wiki does
not publish spawn coordinates or exact appearance times; use the active world and public
rumors as guidance.

## Rare Phoenix

A rare Phoenix can appear every hour in one of Britannia's cemeteries. It is a
special creature and cannot be tamed.

The spawn avoids areas actively protected by guards. See [Cities and
guards](../mundo/cidades-guards.md) for confirmed Felucca exceptions.

Carving its untouched corpse can award **Fire Feathers**. The bonus depends on
Forensic Evaluation; a higher effective skill increases the chance of receiving
a bonus over the normal yield.

## Rare Britannia

The world maintains one active wild **Rare Britannia** mount at a time in an
accessible area of pre-T2A Felucca wilderness. It appears with one of the
available families and color variations and requires **90.0 Animal Taming** to
tame.

**When it is tamed or dies, replacement starts without a scheduled wait**, as
soon as a valid location is available for the new mount. There is no 12-hour wait.

The **12 hours are the maximum time a mount remains wild**: if nobody tames
or kills it within that time, it disappears and replacement begins. This is
not an interval between spawns and does not apply to an already tamed mount.

## Nochyer, the Darkness Beneath

Nochyer may emerge northeast of Yew after long intervals. Black Moon marks an
area before striking, and the encounter holds an interaction for players to
discover. Nochyer is always at least Legendary.

Defeating him may grant Heart of the Black Moon, Black Moon Aegis, three Tomes
of Expertise, one Scroll of Knowledge, an additional Loot Pack roll, and a lore
book with a 10% drop chance.

## Odran, the Red Verdict

Odran may emerge in Wrong's deepest corridors with Seris and four daemonic
witnesses. The ground is marked for one second before Sentence of Silence;
Verdict repeats every 20 to 25 seconds. After five seconds in Paralyze, Odran
teleports out of the field.

Defeating him releases a tamable Ruby Mustang protected from wild creatures for
60 seconds and may award The Condemned Heart, extra PvM rewards, his lore book,
and The Ruby Judgment scythe.

## Fixed events

The fixed events are separate from Rare Britannia: the **Rare Rideable Llama
requires 60.0 Animal Taming**, while **Desert Ostard, Forest Ostard, and Frenzied
Ostard require 70.0**. They appear at fixed world locations; finding them
remains part of exploration, without publishing coordinates.

For the leads shared by Town Criers, see [Whispers of
Britannia](whispers-britannia.md).

## Respawn times

| Event | Interval | How to read it |
|---|---|---|
| Rare Phoenix | 1 hour | New attempt in the event cycle, subject to occupancy and a valid location. |
| Rare Rideable Llama | 1 hour | New attempt in the event cycle, subject to occupancy and a valid location. |
| Rare Desert Ostard | 2 hours | Independent cycle; an existing wild mount can prevent a new spawn. |
| Rare Forest Ostard | 3 hours | Independent cycle; an existing wild mount can prevent a new spawn. |
| Rare Zostrich Ostard | 5 hours | Independent cycle; an existing wild mount can prevent a new spawn. |
| Nochyer | 3–6 days after death | A random interval is selected after the boss dies. |
| Odran | 3–6 days after death | A random interval is selected after the boss dies. |
| Rare Britannia | Always one available | The world maintains one active wild mount. When the slot is freed, replacement begins as soon as a valid location is available. |

Hourly cycles are not countdowns restarted by each kill. A cycle can pass
without a new creature appearing; these intervals do not guarantee an exact
arrival time.
