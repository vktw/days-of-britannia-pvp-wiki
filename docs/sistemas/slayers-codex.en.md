# Slayer's Codex

On delivering the first **Bloodmire Bounty**, the character receives the
**Slayer's Codex**. Every defeated creature teaches its Slayer Group, and
each tier bought with gold becomes a permanent point of damage dealt or taken
against that group.

## Bindings in 1.2.0

Rare boss and season books are now **bindings**: with no text, they give
their cover and color to a runebook, the Hunter's Compendium, or the Slayer's
Codex. They drop at **5%**, in **20** covers.
