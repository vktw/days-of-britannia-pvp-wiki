# Slayer's Codex

On delivering the first **Bloodmire Bounty**, the character receives the
**Slayer's Codex**: a blessed book bound to its owner (1 stone,
double-click opens it). If it cannot be granted at that moment, a later
Bounty grants it. Every defeated creature teaches its Slayer Group, and each
tier bought with gold becomes a permanent point of damage dealt or taken
against that group.

## How it works

Each eligible kill adds the kill's final PvM Mastery XP award to that
creature's group track, across the **7** groups (Undead, Humanoid, Reptilian,
Abyss, Elemental, Arachnid, and Fey). Tier n needs **1,000 + 500 per n** XP
and costs **1,000 × (earned tiers + 1)** gold in the backpack or bank, with
no tier cap.

Each bought tier becomes **1** permanent point, sealed on the spot into
**Attack** or **Defense**, your choice:

- **Attack**: **+10%** of your damage against the group on the first point, **+0.5** per further point, no cap.
- **Defense**: **+10%** less damage from them on the first point, **+0.25** per further point, up to **90%**.

## The book

Double-clicking opens the codex in Portuguese or English (with a language
button): the index shows per group the sealed tier, the XP to the next one,
the current percentages, and how many tiers are ready to seal; each group has
its own pages to seal one tier at a time into Attack or Defense, with the
effect and the price. Every hit changed by the Codex prints a light-green
combat line, on by default and switchable off in the book itself.

## Bindings in 1.2.0

Rare boss and season books are now **bindings**: with no text, they give
their cover and color to a runebook, the Hunter's Compendium, or the Slayer's
Codex. They drop at **5%**, in **20** covers.
