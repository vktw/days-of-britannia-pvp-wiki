# Hall of Echoes

Every Sunday, the **Hall of Echoes** opens a solo PvM challenge: book one of
the **24** hours in `.events` and face **4** waves and **6** echoes drawn from
**10** of the shard's bosses. It is one run per account; dying only ends the
run, with nothing lost.

## How to take part

Bookings open on Saturday in `.events`: pick a Sunday hour and show up for it.
Everyone faces the same **6** echoes that day, with a new theme each week. All
the run's gold goes straight to the bank, with a **100%** bonus, and each stage
earns Kharag points (**50** per wave, **100** per echo, and **200** for
finishing). One hour before it opens, the event is announced in the game and
on Discord.

## Rewards

Each Sunday's champion takes a trophy and the title **the Echo Champion**. The
podium earns medals for the talisman slot: **gold** for the first, **silver**
for the second, and **bronze** for the third.

Every creature defeated in the Hall has a **0.5%** chance to leave, straight
into the backpack, one of these items in the event's colour: the **Dye of the
Echoes**, a decorative skull, or the **Helm of the Returning Echo**, a Dragon
Helm of Invulnerability with a random Mastery at **+10%**.
