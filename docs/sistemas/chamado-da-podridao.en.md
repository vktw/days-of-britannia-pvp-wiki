# The Rot's Call

The **Rot's Call** is an event that rises on Felucca's surface and grows with
the players' kills. Every hour, the most hunted place becomes the Call; anyone
who enters the area faces open PvP, richer creatures, and, at the highest level,
the **Rot King**.

## How the Call rises

Every hour, the **32×32**-tile area on Felucca's surface with the most eligible
kills becomes the Call. The announcement gives no coordinates: it tells only the
nearest region and the names of up to **three** characters who killed at least
**10** creatures there in that hour.

Those inside learn first; the public announcement and the Discord one come
**2** minutes later. The area has a **24**-tile radius from its centre, and PvP
is open as everywhere in Felucca outside the towns.

## Levels and bonuses

Inside the area, creatures give more Mastery XP, more Loot Packs, and more gold
as the Call's level rises:

| Level | Mastery XP | Gold |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | +75% | +5% |
| 1 | +100% | +10% |
| 2 | +125% | +20% |
| 3 | +150% | +30% |
| 4 | +175% | +40% |
| 5 | +200% | +50% |

Level **5** uses the same bonus as Fire and Ice, Loot Packs included. The rot
worms are always nearby.

The Call rises **1** level an hour when players reach the kill goal inside it,
and it never cools. The goal follows the shard's activity: **20** kills per
unique IP online, by the mean of the last **24** hours, with at least **3** IPs.
Extra kills count toward the next level, and those inside are told when the
hour's goal is reached.

## The climax

At level **5**, the **Rot King** wakes at the next hour's turn, with a warning to
all **15** minutes before. The climax brings waves of worms, the
**Tombrenders**, and, last, **Grummoth**, the Rot King. Only his death ends the
Call.

In the climax, only those inside the Call can harm its creatures, the brood's
feeders and the tentacles included: blows, arrows, spells, Arcana, poisons, and
pets from outside deal no damage. The climax's creatures do not leave the area
either.

Outside the climax, a kill counts for the Call's level and gives its gold and
Loot Packs only when you and the creature are inside the area. The Mastery XP
bonus also asks both inside.

## Rewards

The worms can rarely leave two wall trophies, worm jars and animated eggs, and
the **Rot King's Bile** dye. **Grummoth** can leave the **Shroud of Rot** (+**5**
Str, Dex, and Int, dyeable), the **Carapace of the Brood**, an Invulnerability
shield with **Infestation** against the creatures that strike you, and the Rot
King's binding. Whoever hurt Grummoth earns the title **the Rotcleaver**.

## Interface and terrain

With the updated DoB client, the world map shows the Call as soon as it is told
to all: its dashed area, the flame at its centre, and a label with its level,
state, and progress. Inside the area, a buff bar icon shows the bonus.

The Call's edge also shows on the ground: bile pools, mushrooms, and bones you
can walk over. All of it goes when the Call ends.
