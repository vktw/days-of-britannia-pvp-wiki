# Tailoring

## 0.43.0 update

**Studded Leather Armor** is no longer craftable, listed in the Tailoring menu,
or sold by NPCs. Existing pieces and buyback remain preserved.

## 0.42.0 update

The **DoB Cloth** robe stat bonus is restored on reconnect when the robe remains
equipped. Storing the robe grants no bonus.
